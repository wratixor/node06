Geno-Dice is an open entry point into connected game meanings here, not a separate isolated wiki. Arena's working catalogue is the Core16 [skills](md://skills-en): cube faces choose an action but do not change the fact that the ability belongs to the character. Higher-tier faces place an action in [ultimate](md://ultimate-en) mode.

The current public mechanics screen lives in [Arena](https://arena.geno-dice.com/skills). This static point keeps the structure and semantic links without freezing balance coefficients that can change.

Polarity is neither two kinds of beings nor a judgment of a person. In the field's root layout, outward, bright and initiating movement is read as yang; inward, holding and receptive movement as yin. The black and white taijitu contain a small seed of the opposite: no manifestation is pure or motionless.

The symbolic words “masculine” and “feminine” here describe only a voluntary reading of one human whole, never a body, a right or a person's worth. They therefore do not become separate points and do not label the map's axes. A future social layer may show its own, possibly different, distribution of meanings.

The present root coordinates are a reviewable semantic seed version, not a community vote. For examples, see [outward movement](md://outward-en) and [inward holding](md://inward-en); the visible map remains a [field](md://field-en) without scale labels.

Stage 1. One space, different entrances

NODE06 connects meanings rather than splitting a site into language editions. A [point](md://point-en) can hold a thought, a note, a link or a longer text. Russian and English texts are independent points; any connection between them is explicit.

Within one server, connected sites share the graph and accounts. A site supplies an entrance, presentation and roots; it does not create another world or database. Users do not need to own a site to author points through the connected service. Subscribing to a point means supporting it; subscribing to an author means trusting that person. Neither follows automatically from a semantic link.

The current source includes an editor, subscriptions, revision proposals, trust and social geometry. These are engine capabilities, not a claim that every independent copy has installed them. Independent servers retain separate registrations and influence; automatic profile synchronization between them is not implemented yet.

The result: a home page can switch labels and navigate to different language points while keeping one graph and stable addresses. The next stage connects an entrance to an existing website.

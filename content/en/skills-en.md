A skill is a coherent character ability: its whole has one meaningful coordinate, while closer examination reveals overlapping components. In Arena, every Core16 skill has an ordered stat triple: the primary stat drives the action, the support stat develops it, and the auxiliary stat refines the result.

All 16 skills are placed in the eight used triples, with two abilities in each: [Body · Onslaught · Reaction](md://triple-body-onslaught-reaction-en), [Body · Self-control · Reaction](md://triple-body-self-control-reaction-en), [Body · Technique · Onslaught](md://triple-body-technique-onslaught-en), [Body · Technique · Self-control](md://triple-body-technique-self-control-en), [Spirit · Onslaught · Reaction](md://triple-spirit-onslaught-reaction-en), [Spirit · Reaction · Self-control](md://triple-spirit-reaction-self-control-en), [Spirit · Technique · Onslaught](md://triple-spirit-technique-onslaught-en), and [Spirit · Technique · Self-control](md://triple-spirit-technique-self-control-en).

This is a catalogue map, not a claim that every triple is already a finished meta build or a separate class. A skill connects to [Geno-Dice](md://geno-dice-en) and can manifest in [ultimate](md://ultimate-en) mode.

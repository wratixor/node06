In current Arena, an ultimate is neither a separate ability nor a fourth skill. The same Core16 face executes in Ultimate mode at tiers IV–VI, and each ability gains its own stronger effect. Overload is a separate enhancement of an already ultimate action when the battle state permits it and the required resource is available.

An ultimate therefore remains connected to its source [skill](md://skills-en) instead of replacing it with a new name. Formula versions and exact costs belong to Arena's public contract; this point records the distinction between an ability's meaning and its current battle manifestation.
